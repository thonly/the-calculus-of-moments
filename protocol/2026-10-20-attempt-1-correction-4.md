## 1. Readings that change (each is a row read as its line, or a scope made explicit)

1. **A43, a clear mind-door object.** The row says registration follows. A45 bars registration for a being outside the sense sphere, for a non-sense-sphere javana and for a non-sense-sphere object, so `a43_clear` as written forced a contradiction for any fine-material being with a vivid mind-object. **The rule:** registration follows a clear mind-door object *for a being of the sense sphere whose javanas and object are of the sense sphere*. (The same shape as correction 3.)
2. **A24, the rate.** The first encoding said *every* stretch of the day-reckoning one snap long holds a trillion cittas. With "no citta lasts a night" that forces a stream with no last moment, which contradicts Protocol 1 §4's occurrences (a stream that ends at an arahant's last moment; a cessation attainment). **The rule:** *any trillion consecutive cittas with no cessation moment among them arise within one snap*; and a snap is a positive length shorter than a night, a night no longer than a week.
3. **A120 and K.** A120 says kamma-born matter arises at all three sub-moments of every citta of an embodied being; K stops it sixteen steps before the death citta. Read literally they collide. **The rule:** A120's second clause holds *except for the cittas within sixteen steps before a death citta*, where K governs.
4. **A39, the sense sphere.** The first encoding made every sense-sphere being have exactly the six bases. The line (§65 `abh07t.nrf.xml:1117`) says all six are *found* in the sense world, and the womb-born have three decads at rebirth; a blind-born being is not a contradiction. **The rule:** a sense-sphere being has *at most* the six bases, and *some* sense-sphere being having all six is an OCCURRENCE.
5. **A46, the arahant.** The first encoding let an arahant have a path citta. The Saṅgaha (§43 `:1433`, §48 `:1453`) gives the arahant 44 vīthi-cittas, of the supramundane only the arahatta fruition. **The rule:** an arahant's supramundane javana is the arahatta fruition and nothing else. (A literal fix, not a new reading.)
