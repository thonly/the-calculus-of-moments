A118 and A120 are more than a row with a rival. They are **two live rows whose headlines contradict each other**:
Ānanda's literal scope forbids kamma-born matter at a citta's dissolution, and Buddhaghosa's own voice (Vism XX §700)
has it arise there. Entered together, they would produce a contradiction by entry. That is a known disagreement between
teachers, which the axiom list already records. It is not a finding.

## 2. The rules that replace Protocol 1 §6

1. **The base is the row's headline:** its first statement, minus any clause the row marks OURS.
2. **A reading the row REPORTS** (*keci*, *apare*, *vadanti*, *kecana*, "the teachers' view") enters only as a variant.
   It does not enter at all if a source in the same row rejects it (*asāra*, *tayidamakāraṇaṃ*). The dissents that
   A41 and A42 report and the Vibhāvinī rejects are not encoded.
3. **Where a row records that its OWN scope is disputed (A118, A124, A125, and A5 with A9), the base is the COMMON
   CORE**, meaning what every recorded reading accepts. Each reading's further scope is a variant, entered at its own
   tier. For A118 the core is: in the course of existence, no mind-born matter arises at a citta's dissolution-moment,
   and in the formless world no truth of suffering arises then. For A125 the core is: a person of entirely black states
   is found in the world, and is sunk now.
4. **Where two live rows are rival lines (A118's literal scope and A120), the base keeps the line the tradition's own
   rule keeps.** The Dhammasaṅgaṇī's sub-commentary rules that a reading which contradicts neither the Pāli nor the
   commentary is to be taken (`abh01t.tik.xml:2553`, Protocol 1 §3). A120 is the commentary's, in Buddhaghosa's own
   voice. Ānanda's literal scope is a sub-commentary's, and the anuṭīkā rejects it. So A120 is base and Ānanda's scope
   is a variant. The variant's model is expected to fail against A120, and it is reported as a **clash between
   teachers**, never as a finding of the attempt.
5. **A clause a row marks OURS is never an axiom.** If the build needs it (for example A42's fit rule extended to every
   stage, K's count including the death citta, or A49's 360-day divine year), it is a declared choice, listed with the
   freedoms.
6. **Variants are tested one at a time** against the base, each in its own model. A5 and A9 are one dispute and enter
   together as one variant.

## 3. What layer L5 holds

Protocol 1 §5 named only the Vibhāvinī at L5. Several rows also cite Ledi Sayadaw's Paramatthadīpanī and Anudīpanī.
These are later commentaries on the same manual, so **L5 is the manual's commentaries: the Vibhāvinī, and Ledi's
Paramatthadīpanī and Anudīpanī.** A clause from any of them enters at L5.
