# Amendment 3 to Protocol 1 — the double encoding gates on a resolution log, not a rate

*Amends Amendment 1's R2 (the double encoding: its scoring and its threshold), none of whose files is edited. Committed and timestamped before the encoding is frozen (Protocol 2) and before any model is searched. A change made AFTER two failed rounds, and stated as that.*

- **Written by:** Miss Aquarius℠
- **Approved by:** Thon Ly, 2026-10-21 (*"do per your recommendation"*, on the recommendation to gate the freeze on a recorded resolution of every disagreement)
- **Evidence:** `SiliconWat/formal-abhidhamma` `model/DOUBLE-ENCODING-RESULT.md` (round 1: 4 of 25 AGREE, block 972410) and `model/DOUBLE-ENCODING-RESULT-2.md` (round 2: 2 of 25 AGREE, block 972508, pre-registered with the unrepaired Cond rows named as the expected risk). In both rounds two independent adjudicators, shuffled and blinded, gave the same verdict on all 25 pairs: the instrument is reliable.

## 1. What changes

Amendment 1's R2 made **at least 20 of 25 pairs logically equivalent** the condition for freezing. Two rounds show that two blind encoders reading a multi-clause row over a 400-line vocabulary reach logically equivalent Lean in a small minority of cases (4/25, 2/25), however the vocabulary is documented and however many rows are repaired. The rate measures the strictness of logical equivalence, not whether the encoding is idiosyncratic. **The rate is kept as a reported number and stops being the gate.**

## 2. The new gate (all four hold before Protocol 2 freezes)

1. **Every non-agreeing pair of every round has a recorded resolution** in `model/DOUBLE-ENCODING-RESOLUTIONS*.md`, one of: **(i) ours is wrong**, repaired (the repair passes the forbidden-world suite and the blind reader's English is recorded); **(ii) ours stands**, with the reason stated against the row's own words — ⛔ *the encoder dropped a clause* is a reason only if the clause is quoted from the row; **(iii) the row is ambiguous**, declared as a freedom in the freedoms list and carried to Protocol 2.
2. **The freedoms list** (every (iii), every reading the founder ruled) is read and approved by the founder, in a dated file, with the table.
3. **A repaired row is not a sample.** A row repaired after a round is out of any later draw as a clean test; its repaired definition gets a fresh blind re-read, as every definition does.
4. **Nothing else changes:** the 127 rows, the classes, the layers, the controls, the deferred findings and the `/novelty` FULL census stay as Protocol 1 and its corrections 1–4 and Amendments 1–2 have them. A third round may be run for information and is not a gate.

## 3. What this does not say

It does not say the failed rounds passed or reclassify them: both results stand, as measured, in the record. It does not say the encoding is right; a resolution log is a record of reasoning, and the founder reads it. It does not remove the double encoding's finding that a second honest reader reaches a different formalization of a row in most cases — it makes that finding the thing Protocol 2 must carry, row by row, instead of a rate it cannot reach.
