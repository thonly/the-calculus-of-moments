## 1. The row and the line

A42 grades an object's intensity by how many of its moments are left when it enters the door. For the **very great** grade the
row says "the full process, with registration". The Saṅgaha's line (ch. 4 §10, `abh07t.nrf.xml:1237`) says the two
registrations come **as fitting** (*yathārahaṃ*), and A45 bars registration outside the sense sphere (§29–30,
`abh07t.nrf.xml:1353–1361`). Read as the row's words, a being of the fine-material sphere can have no very great five-door object:
the grade would demand a registration that A45 forbids it. That is a collision of the row's words, not of the text.

**The rule:** for this attempt, A42's very great clause reads "the process reaches the javanas" (`a42_very_great`: at least one
javana when one past moment is left). Registration is A41's order and A45's scope, not A42's. The old forbidden world, a
seven-javana process with no registration, is now a world the grade admits (`a42_admits_veryGreatBare`); the new forbidden world is a very
great object whose process never reaches a javana (`a42_forbids_veryGreatNoJavana`). `kala/AXIOMS.md` carries a dated note on the row.
